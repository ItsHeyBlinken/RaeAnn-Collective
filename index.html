Our Story

Rae Ann Collective brings together a mother–daughter team with more than 25 combined years in the wedding industry. What began as a passion for helping couples celebrate without stress grew into two complementary businesses serving the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

Through Stress Free Events & Weddings, we offer full wedding planning, 90-day planning, and Wedding Club guidance—so couples can join our stress-free couples and be guests at their own celebration. Our planners handle vendors, timelines, and every detail across the DFW metro.

Along the way, we discovered bridal gown designers not fully represented in North Texas—and opened Exquisite Bride, our bridal boutique in McKinney, to bring couture collections from designers like Anna Campbell, Eddy K, Elissar Bridal, and Zakaa Couture to local brides.

Whether you need a planner, a gown, or both, our brands work together to make your wedding journey seamless from first consultation to final walk down the aisle.
