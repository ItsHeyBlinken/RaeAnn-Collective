# Project Brief: Rae Ann Collective

## Overview
Single-page directory website at **raeanncollective.com** serving as an umbrella brand hub for wedding businesses associated with Rae Ann Collective.

## Goals
1. **Brand hub** — Explain how Exquisite Bride and Rae Ann Weddings relate under one family of DFW wedding businesses.
2. **Backlink anchor** — Provide crawlable, contextual outbound links to child brand sites for SEO entity association.

## Child Brands
- **Exquisite Bride** — Bridal boutique in McKinney, TX ([exquisite-brides.com](https://exquisite-brides.com/))
- **Rae Ann Weddings** — Wedding planning & coordination in DFW (rebrand of Stress Free Events; target domain raeannweddings.com)

## Success Criteria
- Clear single-page experience on mobile and desktop
- Descriptive anchor text links to both brand sites
- Organization schema linking collective to sub-brands
- Deployable via Docker/nginx on Coolify

## Backlink Graph
- **Outbound (this site):** Links to Exquisite Bride and Stress Free Events / Rae Ann Weddings
- **Inbound:** Rae Ann Weddings redesign footer links back on staging; **Exquisite Bride inbound link** is an external task for their web team (no repo access)
